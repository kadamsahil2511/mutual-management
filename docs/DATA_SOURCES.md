# Data sources and limitations

## Mutual fund reference data

The six direct-growth scheme references are a snapshot dated 31 August 2026. SBI figures are from the [SBI Mutual Fund August 2026 factsheet](https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/scheme-factsheets/all-sbimf-schemes-factsheet-august-2026.pdf?sfvrsn=2110e2d5_4); HDFC figures are from the [HDFC Mutual Fund August 2026 factsheet](https://files.hdfcfund.com/s3fs-public/2026-09/HDFC%20MF%20Factsheet%20-%20August%202026.pdf). Expense, AUM, manager details, minimums, benchmark names and returns are dated source values; the `datedFundReturns` values are Direct plan growth returns where the factsheet published them, paired with the first-tier TRI benchmark. Current NAV history is fetched separately by scheme code.

`expenseRatioPct` stores disclosed Direct BER, not full TER. SBI reports BER excluding GST and TER adds brokerage, transaction costs and statutory levies (including GST). HDFC describes its BER as including statutory levies on the expenses comprising BER but excluding brokerage, transaction costs and execution-related statutory levies. The two definitions differ. The `expenseBasis` field travels with each number; do not average these values or present them as a like-for-like cross-AMC comparison. SBI Direct TER values in the source are Flexicap 1.27%, Corporate Bond 0.37%, and Aggressive Hybrid 0.73%; the complete comparable HDFC TER figure was not found in the downloaded factsheet. HDFC figures stored are the source's Direct BER: Flexi Cap 0.67%, Aggressive Hybrid 1.06%, Corporate Bond 0.38%.

HDFC minimum SIPs are ₹100, checked on the official product pages on 5 October 2026: [Flexi Cap](https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds//hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/direct), [Corporate Bond](https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-corporate-bond-fund/direct), and [Aggressive Hybrid](https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-aggressive-hybrid-fund/direct). They are labeled with that separate current-page date, not the August snapshot. Their factsheet purchase minimum is ₹100. SBI minimum lump-sum and SIP amounts come from each scheme's Minimum Investment section in the August factsheet. HDFC manager experience/other schemes come from the factsheet manager-wise scheme annexure; SBI experience comes from each scheme's manager table. SBI's factsheet reports Anup Upadhyay's two-scheme count but does not name the second fund, so it is left blank. SBI Corporate Bond and SBI Aggressive Hybrid each explicitly name Rajeev Radhakrishnan, so each lists the other scheme under `managerOtherFunds`.

Holdings are partial and dated 31 August 2026. Each included line comes from the named scheme's top holdings in the August factsheet: HDFC Flexi Cap (printed pp. 7–8), SBI Flexicap (p. 15), HDFC Aggressive Hybrid (pp. 48–50), and SBI Aggressive Hybrid (p. 71). Security names/weights are transcribed from those blocks. NSE official equity master ([securities available for trading](https://www.nseindia.com/static/market-data/securities-available-for-trading)) was used to validate equity ISINs against issuer names; sectors use the disclosed issuer industry where available and official exchange/AMC classifications for matching SBI issuers. Each holdings block's `sourceUrl` links to the applicable AMC factsheet. The factsheets disclose top holdings, not complete security lists in this catalog, so `holdingsComplete` is false and coverage is only the sum of recorded positions. Exact-security overlap is meaningful only for these disclosed equity positions. Debt issuer aggregates are omitted because issuer names alone do not identify individual debt securities.

HDFC's official [monthly portfolio disclosure page](https://www.hdfcfund.com/statutory-disclosure/portfolio/monthly-portfolio) lists August 31, 2026 XLSX holdings for all three HDFC schemes. We used the downloadable factsheet top-holdings for equity partial coverage and the NSE master for official ISIN mapping; the HDFC corporate-bond issuer listing is intentionally not converted into holdings without individual debt ISINs.

## Fixed deposits

`assets/data/deposits.json` contains standard general-public retail rates for deposits below ₹3 crore. HDFC Bank rates are from its [official FD rate page](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/fixed-deposit/fd-interest-rate), effective 19 August 2026. SBI rates are from its [official retail domestic term-deposit table](https://sbi.bank.in/web/interest-rates/deposit-rates/retail-domestic-term-deposits), effective 15 December 2025; the page stated last updated 16 June 2026 and retained those rates. Selected months are representative tenures within the published bands. HDFC's >36-to-<55-month band is represented at 48 months; SBI's published bands are represented at 12, 24, 36 and 60 months. Senior citizen rates are excluded. Payout/compounding choice, premature withdrawal and tax affect actual proceeds; listed annual rate alone does not determine maturity value. SIP amounts displayed in planning flows are user-selected simulations; they do not validate an AMC's schedules, debit dates, or live transaction acceptance.

## Learning articles

`assets/data/learning.json` contains short original summaries based on official AMFI investor education pages and SEBI Investor material. Each `sourceUrl` links to the supporting official text. `videoUrl` points to an official SEBI Investor-hosted educational MP4, verified to return `video/mp4` successfully. Videos are linked to the closest matching topic; the expense article points to the official Direct Plans explainer because a separate official TER video was not found.
